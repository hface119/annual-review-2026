Self-assessment

Where I'm Still Getting Better

Honest gaps I'm actively working on
Protecting Director-Level Time
Implementing strategies to safeguard time for strategic initiatives and decision-making. The new lead structure is the structural solution, but the behavioral change takes deliberate unlearning.
Accountability on Handoffs
Ensuring clear responsibility and follow-through when tasks are passed between teams. I set up clear handoff points but need to be more deliberate about closing the loop when I'm not executing.
Communicating Wins Upward
Developing effective methods for reporting successes to senior leadership. I tend to build, ship, and move on. This presentation is one concrete step toward changing that pattern.
